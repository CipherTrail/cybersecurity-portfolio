**Banking Environment Risk Assessment**



**Project Overview**

This project demonstrates a foundational cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional commercial bank. The goal is to identify potential threats, evaluate their likelihood and severity, and prioritize risks based on their overall scores.



**Objectives**

* Identify potential cybersecurity risks.
* Assess the likelihood of each risk occurring.
* Evaluate the potential business impact.
* Calculate risk priority scores.
* Document findings in a risk register.



**Operating Environment**

The fictional bank has on-premises and remote employees, individual and commercial customer accounts, and financial regulatory obligations. The assessment considers threats to bank funds, customer information, financial records, and business operations.



**Risks Evaluated**

1. Business email compromise
2. Compromised user database
3. Financial records leak
4. Theft
5. Supply chain attack



**Risk Scoring Methodology**

* Likelihood: 1 = Low, 2 = Moderate, 3 = High
* Severity: 1 = Low, 2 = Moderate, 3 = High



Risk Priority Score = Likelihood x Severity



The resulting score ranges from 1 to 9. Higher scores indicate risks that may warrant greater attention.



**Deliverables**

* *risk-register.csv:* A structured record of the identified risks and their scores.
* *risk-assessment-report.md:* Explanations of the scoring decisions



**Skills Demonstrated**

* Cybersecurity risk assessment
* Threat identification
* Risk prioritization
* Security documentation
* Basic risk register development
